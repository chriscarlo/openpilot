# Longitudinal Test-vs-Runtime Gap Audit — 2026-07-01

Scope: 2023 Kia EV6 HDA2 CAN-FD lead-following. Question: why do all tests pass while the real car overbrakes, overshoots, undershoots, and occasionally nearly collides during lead follow?

Method: 51-agent audit (runtime pipeline mapping, test-suite audit, 5 gap lenses, 2-vote adversarial verification per finding, repro design), cross-checked against 11 minutes of real engaged lead-follow rlogs pulled from the device (routes 000001f0--4dde6db685, 000001f1--12fea061e5).

## Verified findings (14 confirmed, 3 plausible, 1 refuted)

### [CONFIRMED/high] No closed loop anywhere contains radard's ModelLeadTracker: both simulators fabricate radarState directly, so the ~2.8s dRel EMA lag the EV6's only lead source adds is never measured against a braking lead

On the EV6 every lead is vision-derived and passes through ModelLeadTracker smoothing (dRel tau default 2.8s, model_lead_filter_tau_s, longitudinal_live_tune.py:482,592) before radarState is published. The maneuvers Plant builds radarState by hand with an explicit 'TODO use real radard logic' (plant.py:147-148), and the harness synthesizes radarState from scripted directives (closed_loop.py:318,504+). test_radard_model_lead_filter checks noise suppression open-loop but never asserts step-response lag or its accel consequence. The perception filter that delays recognition of a decelerating lead is therefore exercised by zero ego-feedback tests.

**Symptom link:** Undershoot then near-collision: a gradually braking lead's dRel/vRel reach the MPC seconds late through the tracker EMA (cascaded with the MPC's own Kalman/EMA dRel filter), producing late brake onset followed by panic overbrake — exactly the reported pattern — while every green test feeds the planner clean, unlagged leads.

**Evidence:** selfdrive/test/longitudinal_maneuvers/plant.py:139-153 (hand-built log.RadarState.LeadData; TODO at 147-148); selfdrive/test/longitudinal_harness/closed_loop.py:318,504 (_build_radar_state from LeadDirectives, no radard); selfdrive/controls/lib/longitudinal_live_tune.py:482,592 (model_lead_filter_tau_s default 2.80s); selfdrive/controls/radard.py:225 (ModelLeadTracker applied to every vision lead on this platform)

### [CONFIRMED/high] Both simulators feed the MPC aLeadTau=1.5 while runtime model leads carry aLeadTau=0.3, so simulated lead-accel information decays 5x faster than on the car

extrapolate_lead decays lead accel as a_lead*exp(-tau*t^2/2) (long_mpc.py:2946-2948) — larger tau means faster decay. **Symptom link:** Overbraking: vision aLeadK is noisy; with tau=0.3 a spurious -1 m/s2 aLeadK shapes the entire MPC lead trajectory for seconds, commanding decel the sims (tau=1.5, clean derivative aLeadK) never reproduce. Also masks real-lead-braking undershoot differences between sim and car.

**Evidence:** selfdrive/controls/lib/longitudinal_mpc_lib/long_mpc.py:2946-2951 (extrapolate_lead exp(-tau*t^2/2) decay); selfdrive/test/longitudinal_maneuvers/plant.py:147-148 (aLeadTau = _LEAD_ACCEL_TAU with TODO); selfdrive/test/longitudinal_harness/closed_loop.py:612 (lead.aLeadTau = _LEAD_ACCEL_TAU); selfdrive/controls/radard.py:26,198,486 (_LEAD_ACCEL_TAU=1.5 vs model-lead aLeadTau=0.3)
